Five chapters, one life.

My biodata gives you the facts. This page gives you the person behind them.

Chapter01

Roots

I was born on 28 April 1998 in Karanja (Ghadge), in Wardha district of Maharashtra. My father is a retired teacher and my mother looks after our home. I went to Model High School in Karanja, and our family home is still there.

Chapter02

Learning

After school I did my diploma at Acharya Shrimannarayan Polytechnic in Wardha, and then a B.Tech in Computer Engineering at MIT Academy of Engineering in Pune. Pune is where I studied, and where I now live and work.

Chapter03

Work

Today I work as a software developer at IBM India Software Lab in Yerwada, Pune, on a hybrid schedule. I enjoy writing clean, efficient code, finding creative answers to complex problems, and building digital products together with other people.

Every trip becomes a story.

Chapter04

Beyond the screen

Away from work I love to travel: new places, new food, new cultures. I find joy in small things like a well-brewed coffee, a spontaneous road trip or a book that sparks an idea. To unwind I watch movies, listen to music, and dive into documentaries and podcasts. Now and then I cook and try out a new recipe.

Chapter05

What I believe

I believe in a balanced life, where personal happiness and professional growth go hand in hand. I value honesty, empathy and conversations that go beyond the surface. I am driven by curiosity, guided by integrity, and inspired by the people I meet and the journeys I take.
